Our little story

A café run by cats
(and three tired humans)

Warm cups, fresh bakes, and a lot of fur on the furniture.

Whiskers Café is a small, sunny corner of the neighborhood where good coffee, fresh bakes and gentle cats live under one roof. Everything on the menu is made in small batches each morning, and the cats come and go as they please.

Come to read, to catch up with a friend, to work, or to do absolutely nothing next to something that purrs. Nobody is ever pushed into a cuddle. The cats decide who gets lap privileges.

House rules are short: be gentle, let the cats come to you, and never stand between Peaches and a sunbeam.
